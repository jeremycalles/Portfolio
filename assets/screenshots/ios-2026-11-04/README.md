# iPhone App Store screenshots (1.1.1)

Prepared 4 November 2026 for version 1.1.1. Five numbered screenshots per locale (English and French), in two accepted portrait sizes. `overview-*.jpg` files are review contact sheets, not upload assets.

The headlines and frames are reused from `ios-2026-11-01`. Only the app capture changed, to show the benchmark comparison lines, the new S&P 500 color and the current interface.

Captures use a temporary build with market fetches disabled and a generated database of fictional holdings. No screenshot fixture is in the shipping project, and no real portfolio data was used. These images illustrate features, not investment forecasts.
