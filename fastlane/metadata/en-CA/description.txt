See what you own, how it is performing, and where your money is invested. Track stocks, ETFs, funds, gold and crypto on iPhone and Mac, without creating an account or linking your bank.

MAKE SENSE OF YOUR PORTFOLIO

• Your investments at a glance — See total value in EUR, investment return, and gains or losses for your selected period.
• Performance with context — Explore portfolio value, invested capital and transaction markers. Compare with the S&P 500, MSCI World and gold when market history is available.
• Money added is not money earned — Time-weighted return accounts for recorded cash flows, helping you distinguish investment performance from contributions and withdrawals.
• Every position, explained — Record and edit buys, sells and adjustments. Review quantities, average cost, and realized and unrealized gains when transaction prices are known.
• A clearer allocation — Organize holdings by account and category to see where your portfolio is concentrated.

BRING YOUR ASSETS TOGETHER

Track stocks, ETFs, mutual funds, cryptocurrencies, gold, silver, supported physical coins and manually entered cash balances. Add instruments by ISIN or ticker and organize positions across banks and brokers. Holdings and transactions are entered manually; bank accounts are not connected.

PRIVACY BUILT IN

Your portfolio is stored locally. No Portfolio Vault account, no advertising and no analytics. Price requests send public market identifiers, not your holdings or balances. Hide amounts on screen and enable biometric protection on supported devices. Optional iCloud backup is available when iCloud access is enabled.

Native apps for iPhone and Mac, with English and French interfaces. Browse saved data offline; internet access is needed to retrieve market prices. Prices and historical coverage depend on the source and may be delayed. Background updates depend on system availability.
